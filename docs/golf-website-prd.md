## Hotel operator, booking and investment options

Secure an operator's letter of intent before selling any part of the hotel, because the operator's name and terms drive what investors will pay. These are starting points for your lawyers and financial advisers, as this is not legal or financial advice.

### Ways to raise hotel investment

| Option | How it works | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel company shares (recommended) | One company owns the hotel; a few larger investors buy shares | Simple for operators; one owner to deal with; suits an offer to a small group | Needs valuation and a shareholder agreement |
| Strategic partner | Hotel group or development investor funds in return for a stake | Brings credibility and capital | Gives up control |
| Sell the hotel, keep managing | Investor buys the building; you or the operator run it | Large capital at once | Hard before a track record exists |
| Selling individual rooms | Buyers own rooms and share income | Raises money early from many buyers | Operators are wary; may count as a regulated investment; difficult with DUAT land rights |

Recommended sequence: complete the feasibility study, get an operator letter of intent, then approach a small group of investors privately. Avoid a public offer on the website: taking investment from the public can trigger regulation in South Africa, Europe and Mozambique, so lawyers in each should review the investment pages.

### Booking systems

No booking is needed now. Bookings depend on the operator:

| Scenario | What happens |
|---|---|
| International operator signs | Its own reservation system and website are used; Island Rock links to it |
| You run the hotel | You need a property management system, booking engine and channel manager. Cloudbeds, Mews and SiteMinder are the names to get quotes from |
| Booking.com, Expedia and similar | Register about 6 months before opening, connected through the channel manager so rooms cannot be double-sold |

Restaurant and beach bar open first, so table reservations and tee-time booking can come later as separate tools.
